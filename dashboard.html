About me

Hi, I'm Emily Geguzinskas. I built this weather station in Boca Raton, Florida, to keep an eye on local conditions and the storms that roll through South Florida.

Everything on this page comes straight from my own sensors, updated every few seconds. Use the buttons at the top to look back over the last 15 minutes up to the whole recording, and hover over a chart to see exact readings.

How the station works

  • Brain: an ESP32 microcontroller running MicroPython
  • Temperature & pressure: BMP280 sensor
  • Humidity: DHT11 sensor
  • Rain: a rain-drop plate that senses water on its surface
  • Display: a small OLED screen on the station shows the latest readings

A reading is taken every 2 seconds and sent to this website.
